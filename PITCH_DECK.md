# Pitch Outline: Meeting with Job Network Provider

Use this as a cheat-sheet for your meeting tomorrow to show her why this app is her new best friend.

## 1. The Hook: "Zero-Friction Engagement"
"Traditional job boards are boring and candidates ghost them. Our app uses the Tinder swipe mechanic, which makes job searching addictive and keeps your caseload engaged."

## 2. The Solution: "The Proof of Activity"
"You need to report on candidate activity. Our app automatically logs every swipe (left or right). We can provide you with a dashboard that shows exactly how many jobs your candidates applied for today, with zero paperwork for you."

## 3. The Local Edge: "Melbourne-First"
"We are focusing on Melbourne's high-turnover industries—coffee, retail, and tech. Instead of 2-week wait times, we enable a 'Match & Message' system that gets your candidates into trial shifts within 48 hours."

## 4. The Ask
"I'd love to have you as a 'Charter Partner.' I can give you a 'Provider Dashboard' where you can track your candidates' success and even feature your top vacant roles at the front of the local swipe deck."
