# Research Intelligence — Live Browser Reconnaissance

Gathered 2026-05-26 via browser-use.

## Asuria — Key Findings

### Core Programs
- **Workforce Australia Services** — Mainstream employment services (Dole/JobSeeker)
- **Inclusive Employment Australia** — Disability Employment Services (DES) arm
- **Transition to Work** — Youth employment program (15-24 year olds)
- **Self-Employment Assistance** — Small business setup support
- **Allied Health Professionals** — In-house mental/physical health support for candidates

### The "Mentor" Model
Asuria calls their consultants **"Employment Mentors"**, not case managers.
> *"Your Asuria Mentor will 'have your back' every step of the way."*
The mentor relationship is central to their brand — the app should reinforce this.

### Employer Value Prop
- **Free** for employers ("no cost to you")
- Diverse talent pool: young people, people returning to work, people with disability, First Nations, CALD
- 40+ years operating nationally
- "Employment Experts" who understand specific industries

### VIC/Regional Presence (relevant to app)
- Melbourne CBD, Warrnambool, Shepparton, Seymour, Echuca, Benalla,
  Wangaratta, Beechworth, Bright, Cobram, Corryong, Kyabam
- Strong regional/rural footprint — app should work for non-city job seekers

### Asuria Branding Notes
- Tagline: *"We believe in you"*
- Strong accessibility focus (UserWay widget on site)
- Tone: supportive, person-centred, not corporate

## Seek Job Ads — Data Format (Real Examples)
- Salary: `$70,000 - $75,000 + Superannuation`
- Location format: Suburb + State (e.g. "Homebush NSW", "Melbourne VIC")
- Tags: "Supportive team environment with great culture"
- Structure: Title → Company → Location → Salary → Tags/Perks
