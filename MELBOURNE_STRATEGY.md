# Melbourne Product & Local Strategy

To become the dominant job-swiping app in Melbourne, we must move beyond generic "Job Search" and lean into the city's specific economic and social geography.

## 1. Geographic Hyper-Localization
- **The "Suburb Vibe" Filter:** Job cards should prominently display the suburb (e.g., "Carlton", "Fitzroy", "South Yarra") rather than just "Melbourne."
- **Public Transport Integration:** Since Melbourne's commute is tram/train-centric, add a "Proximity to Metro/Tram" tag on cards.

## 2. Industry Focus: Hospitality & Specialty Coffee
Melbourne is the world's coffee capital. The MVP should have first-class support for:
- **Barista Skills Tags:** "La Marzocco experience," "Latte Art," "Alternative Milks proficiency."
- **Trial Shifts (The "Second Date"):** Instead of just an interview, the "Match" flow should offer a "Book Trial Shift" button—the hospitality equivalent of a first date.

## 3. High-Growth "Melbourne Tech" Sector
- Focus on the Cremorne/Richmond tech hub.
- Integrate with **Meetup.com** or local tech hubs for "Networking Matches."

## 4. Legal & Compliance (Australia)
- **Fair Work Compliance:** Hooks to ensure job ads list the correct Award rates if applicable.
- **TFN/ABN verification:** Simple onboarding step for employers.
