# Concept Refinement — Hi-Hired Mechanics & Systems

## The Core Loop

### Step 1: Post (Employer)
An employer posts a job. Proper job details — not a free-form task card.

**Card fields:**
| Field | Required | Example |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Job title | ✅ | "Barista / All-rounder" |
| Employer | ✅ | "Little Lane Cafe, Tullamarine" |
| Job type | ✅ | Casual / Part-time / Permanent |
| Pay rate | ✅ | "$32/hr + super" or "$65,000/yr" |
| Hours | ✅ | "Sat 8am-2pm" or "Mon-Fri 9-5" |
| Suburb | ✅ | "Tullamarine" |
| Description | Optional | "We're a busy local cafe..." |

**No:** keyword stuffing, SEO optimization, screening questions, application forms, company size dropdown, industry taxonomy.

### Step 2: Browse (Job Seeker)
The job seeker opens the app and sees a deck of job cards. All active jobs in their area, sorted by newest first. No algorithm. No "recommended." Just jobs.

**What the card shows:**
- Job title (big, bold)
- Employer name
- Suburb
- Pay rate
- Job type badge (Casual / Part-time / Permanent)
- Hours / shift summary
- Posted time ("2h ago")

Full transparency: pay, location, and hours are on the card before you swipe.

### Step 3: Swipe (Job Seeker)
- **Right** → "I'm interested in this job"
- **Left** → Skip
- **Tap card** → See full description, employer info, requirements

### Step 4: Match (Bilateral)
Employer opens their "Interested" tab. They see a stack of job seekers who swiped right — each with their profile (name, experience, skills, availability).

Employer swipes right on the candidates they like → **MATCH.** Chat opens.

Both parties had to say yes. No unsolicited recruiter messages. No unwanted applications.

### Step 5: Chat
Direct messaging. No templates. No formalities. Just talk.

Suggested openers:
- Candidate: "Hey, saw your barista role. I've got 2 years experience at Campos. Keen to chat."
- Employer: "Hey Maria, your profile looks great. Want to come in for a trial shift Saturday?"

### Step 6: Hired ✅
Both parties tap **"Hired ✅"** when the role is filled. For the employer: the job is marked filled and removed from the deck. For the candidate: their job search for that role is done. For both: a record of a successful hire.

### Step 7: Unmatch ❌
Either party can unmatch at any point before "Hired." If conversation has occurred, a lightweight confirmation appears: "Are you sure? They may be expecting this." After unmatch, the connection is severed silently.

## Profiles

### Job Seeker Profile
Minimal but meaningful:

- **Name / handle**
- **Suburb**
- **Experience** — short paragraph or bullet list. Not a resume. Just what you've done.
- **Skills / tags** — up to 5: "barista, customer service, cash handling"
- **Availability** — "Weekends, weekday evenings"
- **Work rights** — "Citizen / PR / Visa (details)"
- **Photo** — optional

**No:** education history (unless you want it), career narrative, references, links, portfolios.

### Employer Profile
Lightweight:

- **Business name**
- **Suburb location**
- **About** — short description of the business
- **Verified badge** — after admin verification

## Circles — Local Communities

Circles are hyperlocal communities where employers post jobs and job seekers discover them. Instead of searching by keyword, you join the Circles that matter to you and see everything posted there.

- "Tullamarine & Airport West Jobs"
- "Gladstone Park & Surrounding Areas"
- "Brunswick Hospitality"
- "Melbourne CBD Retail"

**v1:** One seed Circle for the beachhead area.
**v2:** User-created Circles for any community or industry.

Circles replace keyword search. You don't search for "barista near me" — you're in the "Brunswick Hospitality" Circle and the jobs come to you.

## The Reputation System: "Would Hire Again"

After a "Hired" job, both parties get one binary choice:

> **Would you work with them again?** ✅ / ❌

Displayed as:
- Candidate card: *"4 out of 5 employers would hire again"*
- Employer profile: *"3 out of 3 hires would work here again"*

**v1:** Skip. Add when there's enough data for it to be meaningful.

## Monetization

**Job seekers: Always free.** Never charge someone looking for work.

**Employers:**
| Tier | Price | What You Get |
|------|-------|--------------|
| **Free** | $0 | Post 2 jobs per week |
| **Boost** | $5-10 per post | Featured placement in Circle feed for 48h |
| **Unlimited** | $30/month | Unlimited posts |
| **Business** | $75/month | Unlimited posts + verified badge + analytics |

**v1:** Free only. No monetization until validated.
