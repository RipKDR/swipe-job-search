# Hi-Hired — Project Context

This is the living context file for future agent sessions. Keep it concise, current, and decision-oriented.

## Product

**Hi-Hired** — a job finder for casual, permanent, and part-time roles. Like SEEK but rebuilt for humans. Bilateral matching, transparent pay, no keyword algorithms, no ATS black holes, no corporate bullshit.

**Tagline:** *The algorithm is you.*

## Current Decision

Hi-Hired is a mobile job finder where:
- Employers post **proper jobs** (title, type, pay rate, hours, suburb, description)
- Job seekers **swipe through** available jobs in their local area
- **Bilateral matching** — both parties opt in before communication starts
- **Full transparency** — pay, hours, location on every card
- **No algorithm** — all jobs visible, you decide
- **Chat-based connection** — match, talk, hire. No application forms. No cover letters.

**This is NOT a gig economy platform.** It's for casual, permanent, and part-time employment — the same market as SEEK, but with a radically simpler, more human experience.

## Beachhead

**First target:** Tullamarine, Gladstone Park, Airport West and surrounding northern Melbourne suburbs.

Why: Active local employment market, existing Facebook group culture proving demand, not overserved by existing platforms at hyperlocal level.

## Strategic Non-Goals

- Not a gig/task platform (not Airtasker)
- Not a freelance marketplace
- No AI/ML matching — ever
- No keyword search — browse by Circle, swipe to decide
- No charging job seekers — ever
- No resume/CV requirements
- No scraping job boards
- No payments/escrow in v1
- No monetization until validated

## Current Phase

Concept refinement. Direction being finalized. User just corrected course from gig-work framing back to proper job finder.

## Risks

1. **Critical — Cold start.** No jobs → no job seekers. No job seekers → no employers. Must solve before launch.
2. **High — Trust & safety.** Need report, block, and basic moderation before app store submission.
3. **High — SEEK's moat.** SEEK has 20+ years of employer relationships. Competing on UX alone is a long game.
4. **Medium — Legal.** Fair Work compliance, work rights verification (employer's responsibility, but platform facilitates).
5. **Low — Monetization.** Can't price until we know people want it.
